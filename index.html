DeuceMate On the App Store

Tennis scoring on your wrist, and an AI coach’s view of your game.

  • Score on your wrist. Swipe up for your point, down for theirs, left to undo. Tiebreaks, deuce and server rotation are handled without reaching for your phone.
  • Log how each point ended. Tag the cause and the finishing shot, then one tap turns the match into a structured coaching prompt for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
  • See how your game is trending. Double faults, unforced errors, forced errors and winners, tracked across every match on the iPhone companion.

Free on the App Store, with no ads and no tracking.

  • Swift
  • SwiftUI
  • watchOS
  • HealthKit
  • iOS 17
